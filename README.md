Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c) competition **The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head**.

## The entry

A single one-line joke about office life, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My calendar was so full of meetings about meetings that I booked one with myself to do my actual job — and I no-showed.

## How it works

`main.py` is pure Python standard library — no dependencies, no network, no randomness. It writes one fixed, hand-written joke (119 characters, a single UTF-8 line) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), after self-checking the gate: 1–200 characters, non-whitespace, single line.

The joke is built for the published judge brief, in the brief's own order:

1. A real joke with a setup and a turn, about office life.
2. Turns on something anyone who has worked in an office knows: a calendar packed with meetings about meetings.
3. Surprises — the speaker no-shows the one meeting meant to save them, instead of stating a complaint plainly.
4. Lands in one line, every word earning its place, punchline last.
5. Feels new — not the usual "this meeting could have been an email" line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
